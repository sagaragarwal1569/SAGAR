Add your free Gemini key

Sagar uses Google's Gemini to answer. You need your own key. It takes about a minute.

  1. Open aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with a Google account.
  2. Press Create API key and copy it.
  3. Paste it below and press Save key.

The key stays in this browser only. Never share it with anyone. On Google's free plan, what you type may be used by Google to improve its products, so keep private details out.
